Welcome to GeoDev Lab Africa

GeoDev Lab Africa is a community and learning initiative focused on developing the next generation of GIS developers, geospatial analysts, and spatial technology professionals across Africa.

The programme provides participants with practical exposure to Geographic Information Systems, web mapping, spatial data, programming, and modern geospatial technologies.

Apply for the Programme

About the Programme

GeoDev Lab Africa brings together learners who are interested in building practical skills and developing solutions to real-world problems using geospatial technologies.

Participants will learn through practical sessions, assignments, projects, peer learning, and mentorship.

What You Can Learn

  • Geographic Information Systems
  • Spatial Data Analysis
  • Web Mapping
  • GIS Development
  • Remote Sensing
  • Spatial Databases
  • Python for Geospatial Applications
  • Open-source GIS

Who Can Apply?

The programme is open to students, graduates, researchers, GIS professionals, developers, and anyone interested in building practical skills in geospatial technology.
